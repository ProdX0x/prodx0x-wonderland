Les trois prompts ci-dessous consignent les spécifications envoyées à l’outil intégré. La génération est originale ; les fichiers résultants ont été copiés depuis le dossier de génération vers `assets/cinematic/`.

## 1. Vallée — référence : assets/reference-target.jpg

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a NEW ultra-detailed cinematic environment matte painting for the background world of an interactive 3D website. Input image is STYLE AND ENVIRONMENT REFERENCE ONLY, not an edit target. Match its extraordinary photographic fantasy fidelity, monumental vertical karst mountain cliffs, layered blue-violet mist, flowing waterfalls, suspended luxury cream-and-bronze pavilions, delicate pink blossoming trees and magical warm sunset light. CRITICAL: output ONLY THE LANDSCAPE, NO phones, screens, devices, UI, typography, logos, floating text, foreground main walkway or foreground product platforms. The real 3D app will render its own path and devices in front. Wide 3:2 landscape composition, highest practical resolution (at least 1536 wide). View from high in a spectacular canyon with abyss below the viewer. Tall rugged detailed mossy cliff faces on the left edge and right edge, cascading waterfalls plunging deep down. Multiple sharply detailed jagged pinnacles in the far middle distance. Vast blue-lavender cloud-filled valley opens in the central 65 percent. Cinematic pink and apricot golden sunset sky across upper 40%, low warm glowing sun on the RIGHT near horizon (at 82% width, 38% height). Left upper side dramatic slate-blue clouds, luminous peach cumulus on right. Distant reflective sapphire lake far BELOW in bottom third with a path of gold reflected sunset. Luxurious tiny architectural terraced gardens and round champagne-glass domed pavilions perched on distant cliffs, waterfalls, flower-covered ledges, waterfalls reflected in water. Layers of drifting luminous clouds inside valley at many depths. Rich details across entire picture, intricate erosion striations in huge cliffs, convincing lush trees. Color grade photographic fantasy epic: rich deep indigo shadows, silver blue mist, warm gold rim light, apricot-pink sun clouds. Keep center relatively open valley for compositing real 3D devices but STILL rich with distant mountains, lakes and mist, never an empty gradient. IMPORTANT not flat illustration, not pastel lowpoly, not minimal, not cartoon; this must feel like a frame from a very high budget cinematic fantasy film, matching the reference's emotional impact. No dark vignette, no borders. No words or letters anywhere. Save the generated image and return its local file path for use in the project.

Premium high budget cinematic fantasy VFX asset for compositing into a real time mountain sunset landscape. Not cartoon, not pastel geometry, not painted illustration. Leave transparent space around silhouette and thin branches. Save and return local image file path.

Beautiful restrained lens glow, gold sunlight, tiny magical luminous pollen, exceptionally high quality film CGI. Lower part blends smoothly into near-black navy for text readability. At 85 percent height an elegant rounded muted luminous periwinkle blue button with exact white text 'Commencer'. Under it tiny very light gray text 'UN REGARD. UNE AVENTURE.' Leave uppermost 6 percent nearly dark for a physical device's camera island, but no actual status bar or island drawn. Keep all text safely 15 percent inset from sides. No Apple logo, no other logos, no UI outside phone screen, no captions, no outer shadow. Refined quiet Apple-level app design, editorial typography, beautiful lifelike petals, not cartoon or flat vector.
