Iris

A gaze-controlled game. What you look at moves away.

Glimmers drift in the dark, and each one is waiting for its iris. Looking directly at a glimmer pushes it away. To guide it where you want, you need to rest your eyes beside it.

Iris is a game of skill, observation and gaze control.

What you need

  • Iris requires an iPhone on which ARKit face tracking is available.
  • If face tracking is unavailable on the device, Iris displays a gaze unavailable screen instead of starting gaze calibration.
  • The game is played in portrait orientation.
  • For the best tracking, hold the iPhone around eye level at a comfortable distance and use it in steady lighting.

First launch

The introductory screens explain the basic gaze interaction. You can revisit the instructions later through How to Play from the home screen or settings.

Camera permission is requested when Iris first needs the front camera for gaze gameplay.

Calibration

A short calibration sequence adapts gaze control to the way you look at the screen.

You can repeat calibration at any time from Settings → Recalibrate Gaze.

If face tracking becomes temporarily unavailable during play, Iris may pause gaze interaction until tracking is available again.

Free chapters and Full Game

  • Chapters I, II and III — 19 levels out of 82 — can be played without a purchase.
  • Iris — Full Game unlocks Chapters IV to XII with a one-time purchase.
  • The Full Game purchase does not renew.
  • The price displayed for Full Game is provided and formatted by the App Store for your storefront.
  • Restore Purchases is available from the Full Access screen and from Settings.

Promotional access

Temporary promotional access may be granted through an Apple Offer Code.

Iris Promotional Access is not offered for direct purchase inside Iris.

If promotional redemption is available on your device, select Use Access Code from the Full Access screen. Iris then opens Apple’s native Offer Code redemption interface.

Apple handles the redemption process. Iris does not read, store or validate the code itself. After Apple completes the redemption flow, Iris refreshes the StoreKit entitlement and recognizes the temporary access.

When temporary promotional access ends, your progress is kept. Chapters that require Full Game access become locked again unless another valid access entitlement is available.

Unsupported devices

If ARKit face tracking is unavailable on a device, Iris intentionally prevents new gaze-dependent acquisition flows and displays that gaze control is unavailable.

Existing StoreKit entitlements can still be recognized, and Restore Purchases remains available.
