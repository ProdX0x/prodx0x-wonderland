Iris

A gaze-controlled game. What you look at moves away.

Glimmers drift in the dark, and each one is waiting for its iris. Looking straight at one drives it off: to guide them, you have to rest your eyes beside them. Iris is a game of skill and attention. It makes no health promise.

What you need

  • An iPhone with the TrueDepth front camera. Without it, Iris shows an unavailable screen instead of starting.
  • The game is played in portrait only.
  • Hold the iPhone at eye level, an arm's length away, in steady light.

First launch

Four screens explain how it works. You can skip them, and see them again at any time through “How to play”, from the home screen or the settings.

Permission to use the camera is asked at the first level only.

Calibration

A short sequence of fixations teaches Iris the way you look. You can redo it at any time from Settings → Recalibrate gaze.

Tracking needs a few seconds to settle after calibration. If your face leaves the frame, the level pauses and resumes on its own.

Chapters and purchases

  • Chapters I, II and III — 19 levels out of 82 — are playable without any purchase, forever.
  • Chapters IV to XII require full access, which is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no renewal.
  • The price shown is the one the App Store formats for your storefront.
  • “Restore purchases” is available on the full access screen and in the settings.
  • If you have an access code, “Use an access code” opens Apple's official redemption sheet. When it expires, your progress is kept; only the paid chapters lock again.

A question?

Write to contact@steve-s.net. Mentioning your iPhone model and iOS version helps a lot.

Privacy

No camera image is recorded or sent. The details are on the privacy policy page.
