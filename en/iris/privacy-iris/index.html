PRIVACY

Iris privacy

Everything is computed on your device, frame after frame, then forgotten.

Iris uses your iPhone's TrueDepth front camera to estimate the direction of your gaze. It is that gaze which moves the spheres of the game.

No image, no video and no data from your face is recorded. Nothing is sent: Iris contains no network code, no server, no analytics service and no advertising tracker.

What stays on your device

  • your gaze calibration coefficients;
  • your progress through the chapters;
  • your preferences for sound, ambience, vibration and the gaze marker;
  • a flag recording that the explanation has already been seen.

Nothing else. None of this data leaves the device and none of it identifies you. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store. Iris sees neither your Apple Account nor your payment method; it only asks StoreKit whether a valid purchase exists for this device.

No account

No account is needed to play. No sign-in is requested, apart from the one the App Store performs itself for a purchase or a restore.

Data collection

In Apple's sense, “collecting” means transmitting data off the device. Iris collects no data and performs no tracking.
